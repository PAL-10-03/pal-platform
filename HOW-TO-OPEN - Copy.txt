PAL Business Solutions — open the test on your computer and phone

You do not need to write code. Do these steps in order.

1. Download PAL-platform-test.zip.
2. Save it to your Desktop.
3. Right-click the zip file.
4. Click Extract All.
5. Click Extract. A folder named pal-platform opens.
6. Double-click START-PAL.bat.
7. If a window says Node.js is not installed, install the LTS Windows version from the page that opens, click Next through the installer, then double-click START-PAL.bat again.
8. If Windows asks to allow Node.js on private networks, click Allow.
9. Your browser opens to http://localhost:3000.
10. Leave the black window open. Closing it stops PAL.
